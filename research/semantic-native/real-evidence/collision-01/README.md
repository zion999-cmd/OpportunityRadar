# Collision 01 — Relationship Discovery

## Purpose

This experiment performs the first collision between one frozen employer-evidence pool and one frozen pool of public professional-experience reconstructions. It asks whether any demonstrated capability in the person-side evidence has a reasonable semantic basis for helping change the employer's current situation toward an outcome the employer appears to want. It does not evaluate candidates, recommend hiring, or perform conventional matching.

## Evidence Boundary

No web research or other enrichment was performed. Only the frozen files listed above were used. No skills, requirements, tags, profiles, taxonomies, constraint/preference schemas, scores, embeddings, keyword representations, knowledge graphs, or other traditional matching representations were created or used.

## Known Limitation

P001–P012 are concise evidence reconstructions created during acquisition, not untouched raw source text. This collision therefore reasons over prior reconstructions and inherits their omissions, framing choices, and source limitations.
