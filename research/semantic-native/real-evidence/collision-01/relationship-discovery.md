# Relationship Discovery — Collision 01

## 1. Employer Situation

Patch publicly describes an AI-native services operation in which climate strategists combine expertise, data, and software across environmental-resource strategy, sourcing, diligence, purchase, and management. Its evidence describes fragmented and non-standardized data, trust-sensitive decisions, resource-intensive workflows, and time pressure. Present delivery includes dashboards, decks, and analyst workflows; the intended direction includes turning recurring manual strategist work into platform capabilities and exploring conversational interfaces, agents, and still-unknown formats.

The intended change appears to be toward a more unified, repeatable product experience that improves internal and customer workflows while preserving rigor and making AI contributions visible and trustworthy. The dedicated product designer is expected to observe strategist and customer work, frame problems, design and prototype, ship production code, lead delivery, and develop the design language and component system. The evidence does not establish which workflow is the first priority, how much work is already automated, how AI trust is measured, or the current design organization. The stated employment conditions are full-time, on-site work in San Francisco, with a $170K–$240K base range and equity.

## 2. Discovered Relationships

### P004

**Judgment:** worth_exploring

**Relationship:** P004 demonstrates a way of making consequential analysis inspectable without treating maximum disclosure as universally appropriate. That capability may matter as Patch turns expert and AI-assisted work into customer-facing decisions whose support, provenance, and limits must remain understandable.

**Demonstrated Capability:** In a seven-month investigation, the person connected quantitative claims to supporting code, put a readable methodology before the code, made the workflow rerunnable, and sought review of assumptions and clarity. The account also distinguishes cases where sensitive data makes full reproducibility inappropriate. Preparing for scrutiny reportedly produced clearer analysis and additional fact-checking.

**Employer Situation Connection:** Patch describes trust-sensitive diligence based on fragmented data and asks that AI contributions be visible and trustworthy. P004 supplies concrete evidence of designing an accountable path from an outward-facing claim back to analysis, assumptions, and review, while preserving a boundary for sensitive material.

**Evidence:** Person-side evidence establishes the publication and review practices, the reported clarity and fact-checking effects, and the conditional limit around sensitive data. Employer-side evidence establishes a combined data/expertise/software service, trust-sensitive diligence, and an explicit desire for visible, trustworthy AI contributions.

**Implied Meaning:** The person appears able to treat transparency as an operational and interface design problem rather than as a binary promise, and to structure evidence so different audiences can inspect it at useful levels.

**Hypothesis:** This capability could transfer into designing provenance, explanation, review, or audit interactions around AI-assisted strategist work. The particular investigative-publication method is not proposed as Patch's solution.

**Unknowns:** The person's role and decision authority in the project; whether they have applied this capability inside an interactive product; Patch's actual AI workflow, confidentiality boundaries, and trust failures; and satisfaction of the stated on-site, location, availability, interest, compensation, and eligibility conditions.

**Next Investigation:** Ask for one example in which the person designed an interactive decision trail for mixed human and automated analysis, including what was shown, withheld, or reviewable and why.

### P006

**Judgment:** worth_exploring

**Relationship:** P006 demonstrates sustained conversion of dispersed, inconsistently recorded experience into a repeatable learning process with an action loop. That capability may matter as Patch tries to turn recurring expert work into platform-supported practice without losing the perspectives and judgment behind it.

**Demonstrated Capability:** Across an eight-year effort, the person addressed geographically dispersed teams, resistance, inconsistent documentation, poor retrieval, and a missing findings-to-action loop. The process gathered project data, reconstructed events from multiple perspectives, communicated action plans, and stored findings centrally; the case reports roughly 500 retrospectives and participant-reported benefits.

**Employer Situation Connection:** Patch wants to observe manual strategist workflows and decide what should become a platform capability. Its public account also describes fragmented information and a need for repeatability without sacrificing rigor or trust. P006 demonstrates operationalizing situated knowledge across many instances rather than merely documenting a single expert's method.

**Evidence:** Person-side evidence establishes the initial organizational conditions, the implemented process, its multi-year use, approximate volume, and the limited nature of its outcome evidence. Employer-side evidence establishes embedded expert work, fragmented data, manual workflows, and an intended movement toward repeatable platform capabilities.

**Implied Meaning:** The person appears able to elicit multiple accounts of work, create usable organizational memory, and connect reflection to follow-through under adoption constraints.

**Hypothesis:** This capability could help Patch discover which parts of strategist practice are stable enough to support in software and which context, disagreement, or judgment must remain visible. It does not establish product-design execution or that Intel's retrospective process should be copied.

**Unknowns:** How the person personally shaped the work versus reported it; how the process changed decisions in practice; whether the capability extends to product discovery and interface delivery; and satisfaction of the stated employment conditions.

**Next Investigation:** Seek a concrete retrospective-to-system example: what recurring judgment was identified, how variation was represented, what action or tool change followed, and what evidence showed that the representation remained useful.

### P008

**Judgment:** worth_exploring

**Relationship:** P008 demonstrates correction of a uniform system assumption through repeated observation of the people who would use it. That capability may matter where Patch must distinguish genuinely repeatable strategist work from variation that a platform should preserve rather than erase.

**Demonstrated Capability:** Repeated crew tastings showed that the original identical-menu plan did not fit individual preferences. The team changed the food-system design so each crew member could choose a menu. The evidence establishes the feedback-driven decision, but not quantified mission or consumption outcomes.

**Employer Situation Connection:** Patch intends to observe strategist and customer workflows and convert appropriate manual work into platform capability. A key implied tension is repeatability without hiding expert judgment. P008 is direct evidence of using real-user feedback to revise an operational standardization assumption.

**Evidence:** Person-side evidence establishes the initial uniform design, repeated user tastings, the discovered disagreement with the assumption, and the resulting configurable design. Employer-side evidence establishes both internal and customer-facing workflows, direct observation of users, and a platformization objective involving complex expert work.

**Implied Meaning:** The person appears responsive to evidence that uniformity is the wrong abstraction and able to preserve meaningful user variation in an operational system.

**Hypothesis:** This capability could help test where Patch's product should standardize a workflow and where it should expose controlled variation for strategists or customers. Nothing establishes that menu personalization itself supplies a solution pattern for environmental markets.

**Unknowns:** The person's individual contribution to the change; the depth and method of the testing; evidence of handling more complex or trust-sensitive variation; and satisfaction of the stated employment conditions.

**Next Investigation:** Ask for an example where user evidence overturned a proposed abstraction in a more complex system, and how the person decided what became fixed, configurable, or left to human judgment.

### P010

**Judgment:** worth_exploring

**Relationship:** P010 demonstrates designing transparency and human oversight for a sensitive participation system, then observing both user behavior and the operational burden created by the design. That capability may matter for Patch's need to make AI-assisted contributions trustworthy while keeping real workflows workable.

**Demonstrated Capability:** In an exhibition expected to provoke strong reactions, the person helped choose an inclusive prompt, a non-ranking display, and a two-question moderation rule; the rule was later made visible. Staff reviewed 739 submissions and displayed 600, with two rejected as hateful or profane. Users could bypass the intended moderation path, requiring twice-weekly inspection of the full display. The case drew lessons for later installations.

**Employer Situation Connection:** Patch explicitly wants visible, trustworthy AI contributions in internal and customer-facing operational tools. P010 shows attention to how policy visibility, interface form, human review, user workarounds, and maintenance burden interact in a trust-sensitive system.

**Evidence:** Person-side evidence establishes the sensitive context, design and moderation choices, mid-course transparency change, counts, bypass behavior, and resulting staff burden. Employer-side evidence establishes trust-sensitive diligence, AI-assisted interfaces, internal and customer surfaces, and a goal of meaningful workflow improvement.

**Implied Meaning:** The person appears able to design for contested participation, make governance legible, and notice when the physical or interaction design undermines the intended control and creates hidden operations work.

**Hypothesis:** This capability could transfer to designing AI review, exception, disclosure, or feedback mechanisms that people can understand and that operators can sustain. The museum moderation mechanism is not itself a proposed product solution.

**Unknowns:** The person's precise ownership and response to the bypass problem; whether the capability carries into software and AI-mediated decisions; Patch's actual failure and escalation modes; and satisfaction of the stated employment conditions.

**Next Investigation:** Ask the person to redesign this case after observing the bypass behavior, explaining how the interface, policy disclosure, review path, and operator workload would change and how success would be evaluated.

### P012

**Judgment:** worth_exploring

**Relationship:** P012 demonstrates evolving the connection between design and implementation through code-based artifacts and repeated live-project experiments, while making learning travel between projects. That capability may matter in Patch's close design-engineering setting, where the designer is expected to prototype or ship code and grow a design system amid changing product forms.

**Demonstrated Capability:** The person describes introducing modular content and layout, front-end style guides, and code-based wireframes across three projects rather than declaring a complete process in advance. The team recorded effects on time, collaborators, and clients; rough prototypes helped a developer and designer ask better questions; lessons moved to another project and eventually into a monthly all-staff learning practice. The methods remained under refinement.

**Employer Situation Connection:** Patch expects end-to-end work from problem framing through production code, close collaboration with engineering and domain experts, continuing development of a design language and component system, and exploration of interfaces not yet fully defined. P012 demonstrates learning through implementation artifacts while building reusable practice incrementally.

**Evidence:** Person-side evidence establishes the live-project experiments, artifacts used, recorded effects, reported improvement in cross-disciplinary questioning, transfer between projects, and continuing uncertainty. Employer-side evidence establishes production prototyping or coding, rapid cross-functional feedback, component-system work, and uncertain future interface forms.

**Implied Meaning:** The person appears able to use prototypes as instruments for discovering workflow and collaboration problems, and to institutionalize learning without pretending the process is finished.

**Hypothesis:** This capability could help Patch shorten the distance between observing strategist work, testing an interface model, and learning from production-adjacent artifacts. The evidence does not establish mastery of Patch's stack, AI interface design, or its environmental domain.

**Unknowns:** The person's individual authorship and technical depth; whether reported collaboration improvements led to better user outcomes; experience with AI or data-intensive operational products; and satisfaction of the stated employment conditions.

**Next Investigation:** Review one code-based prototype with the person and ask which uncertainty it was built to expose, what changed because of it, and how the learning affected a reusable component or later production decision.

### P003

**Judgment:** uncertain

**Relationship:** P003 demonstrates calibrated intervention in a fragile system: gather discriminating evidence, work within irreversible constraints, stabilize what is necessary, and deliberately leave more extensive change for later. This has a non-trivial semantic connection to introducing automation into trust-sensitive expert workflows, but the occupational and medium transfer is large and the evidence does not establish interactive product work.

**Demonstrated Capability:** Faced with a brittle, friable map and insufficient time for complete relining, the person used humidification, repair, a tested low-concentration consolidant, and modified housings to achieve safe researcher access. Infrared photography corrected a presumed pigment identification. The intervention explicitly prioritized stabilization over complete treatment.

**Employer Situation Connection:** Patch appears to be changing partly manual, expert-led work into software-supported systems in a domain where provenance, uncertainty, and trust matter. P003 shows evidence-sensitive diagnosis and choosing a bounded intervention that improves access without claiming to resolve the whole system.

**Evidence:** Person-side evidence establishes the material risks, time constraint, testing, revised identification, limited treatment, and stabilized-access outcome. Employer-side evidence establishes fragmented data, trust-sensitive processes, changing workflows, and the intended coexistence of human expertise and software.

**Implied Meaning:** The person appears capable of resisting an over-complete intervention, updating a prior belief when instrumentation contradicts it, and designing around preservation constraints.

**Hypothesis:** The underlying capability might transfer to safely sequencing productization: instrument uncertainty, improve access or throughput, and preserve reversibility and expert control while evidence accumulates. Whether that abstraction survives transfer from conservation practice to software product design is not established.

**Unknowns:** Whether the person can express and apply this reasoning in organizational and digital systems; their role in selecting and validating each technique; evidence of collaboration with software builders or users beyond researcher access; and satisfaction of the stated employment conditions.

**Next Investigation:** Present a real Patch workflow with uncertain data and ask the person to identify what they would stabilize first, what they would make reversible, what evidence they would gather, and what they would explicitly defer.

## 3. Rejected Tempting Relationships

### P005 — designer and evolving content system

The redesign, early content-system prototype, and improved proposal quality make P005 superficially attractive, especially beside Patch's design-system and product-surface language. But the established change concerns an agency's own representational website and inbound inquiries. The supplied evidence does not show work with trust-sensitive operational decisions, expert-to-platform translation, or internal/customer workflow tooling. “Designer,” “prototype,” and adaptable content are not enough to bridge that gap.

### P009 — playtesting, scope control, and shipping under constraints

P009 contains genuine evidence that user behavior resolved a design dispute, and it records disciplined scope reduction alongside a concrete shipping compromise. Those capabilities are plausible in almost any product environment. The evidence does not establish a more specific connection to Patch's central tensions—making expert work repeatable, handling fragmented evidence, or exposing AI contributions trustworthily—so generic product transferability was insufficient.

### P002 — moving authority away from a controlling leader

P002 demonstrates learning from failure, distributing decisions to captains, and changing feedback practice. It is tempting to connect that to empowering climate strategists in a product-development loop. The employer evidence, however, does not establish centralized product control, dependency on a leader, or disempowered experts. Making that bridge would invent the employer-side problem that made the demonstrated capability effective.

## 4. Pool-Level Unknowns

- The person files are prior concise reconstructions. They may omit decisive context about individual contribution, collaborators, unsuccessful iterations, and downstream outcomes.
- Several accounts are first-person retrospectives, conference descriptions, or oral histories. Outcome strength ranges from concrete events to practitioner observations and participant reports rather than independent evaluation.
- The employer evidence is public self-description. It does not identify the first workflow to change, actual AI behavior, current failure modes, internal design capacity, decision rights, customer access, or the measures by which trust and productivity are judged.
- No supplied person evidence establishes availability, interest, present location, willingness to work full-time on-site in San Francisco, compensation alignment, or employment eligibility. These remain unknown for every surfaced relationship; no contradiction was supplied.
- A demonstrated capability can justify investigation without establishing that it will transfer across domain, medium, scale, organizational authority, or time.

## 5. Methodological Observations

- The strongest relationships connected a demonstrated way of handling a structural tension to an employer tension: inspectability versus sensitivity (P004), repeatability versus situated knowledge (P006), standardization versus meaningful variation (P008), transparency versus operational control (P010), and learning through implementation versus prematurely fixed process (P012).
- Cross-occupation reasoning was possible when the evidence preserved a decision under constraint and an observed consequence. P003 reached only `uncertain` because its abstraction is meaningful but the transfer distance is not bridged by digital-product evidence.
- Shared design vocabulary was a weak signal. P005 and P009 used prototypes, systems, or user feedback, but their supplied outcomes did not create a sufficiently specific transformation relationship to Patch's present situation.
- Employer-side absence mattered differently from person-side logistical absence. An invented employer dysfunction invalidated the tempting P002 bridge, while missing geography or availability merely remains an actionability unknown.
- The collision supports questions for further investigation, not conclusions about qualification, suitability, interest, or hiring.
