# Relationship Falsification — Collision 01

## P003

**ID:** P003

**Previous Judgment:** uncertain

**Specific Demonstrated Change:** The evidence attributes to Heather Wanser a bounded treatment of a brittle map: she used tested material interventions and modified housing to make it safely accessible to researchers while deferring complete relining. Infrared photography also changed the pigment identification from presumed indigo to Prussian blue.

**Specific Employer Change:** The previous relationship proposed better sequencing of Patch's conversion of manual expert work into software-supported workflows: make a limited change, preserve reversibility and expert control, and defer broader automation until evidence improves.

**Mechanism Bridge:** No operational bridge is established. Conservation testing can determine how a physical object responds to a treatment. The supplied evidence does not show that Wanser can identify software workflow boundaries, represent expert judgment, design reversible automation, or evaluate AI behavior. “Bounded intervention under uncertainty” is the analogy, not a demonstrated cross-domain mechanism.

**Evidence Ownership:** The institutional write-up names Wanser and describes her treatment, so attribution is stronger than a team-only report. It establishes conservation judgment, not the productization judgment claimed by the bridge.

**Counterfactual Specificity:** Replacing P003 with many competent professionals who tested a cautious intervention under constraints would leave the Patch argument almost unchanged. The persuasive force comes from the generic pattern of caution and evidence use rather than the distinctive treatment evidence.

**Actionable Information Gain:** The evidence supplies no non-obvious basis for investigating this person specifically for Patch beyond an interesting analogy. A hypothetical exercise about a Patch workflow would test an unestablished capability rather than deepen an already evidenced one.

**Strongest Falsification Argument:** The previous result translated physical conservation principles into software-product principles without evidence that the person had exercised the intervening decision abilities. The transformation path is metaphorical at its critical step.

**Verdict:** FAILS

**Why:** A specific conservation outcome is attributable, but the capability required to change Patch's state is not. The relationship is primarily an abstract analogy about fragility, uncertainty, and reversibility.

## P004

**ID:** P004

**Previous Judgment:** worth_exploring

**Specific Demonstrated Change:** Jeremy Singer-Vine describes structuring a published investigation so each quantitative statement connected to supporting code, a readable methodology preceded the code, the workflow could be rerun, and assumptions and clarity received review. Preparing the package reportedly caused clearer analysis and additional fact-checking. He also articulated a concrete boundary: sensitive data can make complete reproducibility inappropriate.

**Specific Employer Change:** Patch could make an AI-assisted strategist output inspectable by connecting a presented claim or recommendation to the supporting analysis, stated method or assumptions, and available review path, while withholding material that should not be disclosed. This would change an opaque output into one whose evidentiary path and disclosure boundary can be examined.

**Mechanism Bridge:** The transferable mechanism is construction of a layered claim-to-evidence path and a conditional disclosure decision. Both the past work and the possible Patch intervention require deciding how an audience moves from an outward claim to supporting computation and methodology, how another person can review or rerun that work, and where sensitivity limits disclosure. This does not require copying a journalism publication package into the product.

**Evidence Ownership:** This is a first-person professional project write-up and attributes the concrete linking, ordering, rerunnability, and review practices to Singer-Vine's work supporting the investigation. Some choices and outcomes are described at team level, and the claimed clarity improvement is his report rather than an independent measure, but the source establishes more than passive observation.

**Counterfactual Specificity:** A random professional who merely “worked with data” or “valued transparency” would not supply the same evidence. The claim-level linkage to code, readable methodology layer, rerunnable workflow, external review, and explicit sensitivity exception form a distinctive demonstrated pattern.

**Actionable Information Gain:** The evidence gives Patch a concrete line of investigation that conventional role or industry representations could miss: whether this person can translate claim-level computational accountability and bounded disclosure into an interactive AI-assisted decision surface. The next evidence sought would directly test medium transfer rather than generic competence.

**Strongest Falsification Argument:** The evidence concerns static publication of analysis, not interactive product design or AI outputs, and the source does not fully separate Singer-Vine's decisions from the team's. Patch's actual trust failure and confidentiality rules are also unknown, so the employer may not need this exact intervention ability.

**Verdict:** SURVIVES

**Why:** Despite the medium gap, the evidence establishes a specific set of decisions that changed how consequential computational claims could be inspected and checked. There is a plausible operational bridge to a specific Patch state change, and the relationship depends on evidence more distinctive than generic transparency.

## P006

**ID:** P006

**Previous Judgment:** worth_exploring

**Specific Demonstrated Change:** Debra Lavell describes an eight-year effort that changed retrospectives from inconsistent, poorly retrievable activity without a dependable action loop into a repeated process that gathered project data, reconstructed events from multiple perspectives, created and communicated action plans, and stored findings centrally. The paper reports roughly 500 retrospectives, with benefits supported mainly by practitioner estimates and participant reports.

**Specific Employer Change:** The prior relationship proposed helping Patch convert recurring strategist experience into a retrievable body of evidence from which recurring actions, variations, and follow-up needs could inform platform changes rather than remain scattered across manual delivery.

**Mechanism Bridge:** The established mechanism—eliciting multiple accounts, reconstructing events, recording actions, and retaining findings—could improve Patch's organizational learning about strategist workflows. But the evidence does not show the further mechanism required by the previous claim: determining which expert judgments can be encoded into software, representing their variation, or turning recorded lessons into product capabilities.

**Evidence Ownership:** The acquisition note says the case records sustained responsibility, but the raw reconstruction says Lavell “describes” the effort and repeatedly refers to the adopted process without specifying which decisions she personally made. The scale establishes organizational use; it does not firmly establish her individual causal contribution or judgment at each step.

**Counterfactual Specificity:** The relationship is more specific than generic process improvement because it includes multi-perspective reconstruction, action plans, retrieval, and long-duration adoption under distributed constraints. Still, many professionals who implemented a lessons-learned program could support nearly the same Patch hypothesis; the distinctive evidence does not reach expert-work productization.

**Actionable Information Gain:** It provides a non-obvious reason to investigate whether the person can build an evidence-to-action learning loop around strategist work. It does not yet provide a strong reason to believe she can change Patch's product surfaces or encode expertise, which is the important transformation in the employer evidence.

**Strongest Falsification Argument:** Collision-01 crossed an unsupported step from repeatable retrospectives to repeatable software-mediated expert work. Capturing lessons and closing action items is not evidence of discovering product abstractions or preserving expert judgment in a platform.

**Verdict:** WEAK

**Why:** The source establishes a substantive organizational mechanism beyond generic learning, but individual ownership and the critical transfer from organizational memory to product capability are insufficiently evidenced. Preserve only the narrower hypothesis that this person may help establish an evidence-to-action loop around workflow observation.

## P008

**ID:** P008

**Previous Judgment:** worth_exploring

**Specific Demonstrated Change:** Repeated crew tastings contradicted the identical-menu assumption, and the team changed the food system so each crew member could select a menu. The evidence establishes the decision sequence but provides no quantified effect after the change.

**Mechanism Bridge:** The only established mechanism is that users expressed different preferences during testing and the team allowed individual selection. The evidence does not show diagnosis of complex workflow variation, selection among competing abstractions, consequences of configurability, or preservation of expert judgment in a repeatable platform.

**Strongest Falsification Argument:** Collision-01 inflated a simple preference-driven team change into a capability for locating the boundary between platform standardization and expert discretion. Neither the required judgment nor individual ownership is established.

**Verdict:** FAILS

**Why:** The relationship is generic feedback and configurability similarity. The evidence-specific content does not survive the domain crossing or support person-level attribution.

## P010

**ID:** P010

**Previous Judgment:** worth_exploring

**Specific Demonstrated Change:** Jessica Keyes and a colleague designed a visitor-response station with a non-ranking display and a two-question moderation rule, then made the rule visible after three weeks. The installation processed 739 submissions and displayed 600. Its physical design allowed visitors to bypass moderation, which forced staff to inspect the full display twice weekly and exposed a change needed in later stations.

**Specific Employer Change:** A related Patch intervention would make the governance of an AI-assisted output visible and align the interaction path with its review policy so users cannot unknowingly or easily bypass intended human oversight, while keeping the review workload operationally sustainable.

**Mechanism Bridge:** The past case joins four concrete elements: a sensitive contribution surface, an explicit admissibility rule, disclosure of that rule, and observation that interface affordances could defeat governance and create recurring review work. Those decision dimensions can transfer to an AI-assisted operational surface even though the physical Rolodex and its moderation rule do not. However, the employer evidence does not establish that Patch currently has bypassable review controls or moderation-like decisions.

**Evidence Ownership:** The first-person case attributes design choices to Keyes and a colleague, so joint design responsibility is supported. Staff performed the reviews, and the reconstruction does not establish that Keyes corrected the bypass or personally caused a downstream redesign. Counts are concrete, but interpretations are her informal review.

**Counterfactual Specificity:** A random professional who “designed for trust” would not necessarily bring evidence about the interaction among visible rules, physical affordances, circumvention, and operator burden. The case is evidence-specific. Its relevance weakens because Patch's actual AI governance and failure modes are unknown.

**Actionable Information Gain:** The relationship offers a non-obvious investigation into whether the person can design policy-visible controls whose interface implementation and operating burden agree. That information would be useful if Patch has human-review or exception paths; the frozen evidence does not establish that prerequisite.

**Strongest Falsification Argument:** Collision-01 may have substituted “sensitive context plus transparency” for transfer. The case concerns public-expression moderation, while Patch describes trustworthy AI only at a high level. Without evidence of Patch's review, exception, or circumvention problem, the claimed employer transformation may be invented rather than discovered.

**Verdict:** WEAK

**Why:** The person evidence contains a specific governance/interface/operations interaction and credible joint ownership, so it is more than generic trust similarity. But the Patch-side state and the person's ability to resolve the observed failure are both missing, leaving the transfer as a conditional hypothesis.

## P012

**ID:** P012

**Previous Judgment:** worth_exploring

**Specific Demonstrated Change:** Mark Llobrera describes a team introducing modular content and layout, front-end style guides, and code-based wireframes across three live projects while recording effects on time, collaborators, and clients. Rough prototypes changed the questions a developer and designer could ask; lessons moved into another project and then into a monthly all-staff practice. The process remained under refinement.

**Specific Employer Change:** Patch could reduce the distance between product design and implementation by using code-based prototypes and reusable front-end artifacts to expose product and workflow uncertainties earlier, carry validated interface decisions into production, and evolve its component system across internal and customer surfaces.

**Mechanism Bridge:** Both situations directly involve digital product work at the design-development boundary. The demonstrated mechanism is not iteration alone: implement a rough representation in the medium of delivery, use it to reveal questions that static handoffs did not expose, observe its effects across live projects, and carry useful artifacts and lessons forward. That can plausibly contribute to Patch's explicitly stated production-prototyping and design-system direction.

**Evidence Ownership:** This is Llobrera's first-person process retrospective, but most actions and effects are attributed to “the team” or agency. His individual authorship, coding depth, and decision authority are not specified. The evidence supports participation in the practice more strongly than sole responsibility for its effects.

**Counterfactual Specificity:** Replacing P012 with any professional who merely collaborated or iterated would materially weaken the case. Code-based wireframes, front-end style guides, modular systems, cross-project trials, and recorded effects are specific. Replacing it with another practitioner who had performed the same concrete work would preserve the argument, as it should for capability evidence.

**Actionable Information Gain:** It supplies a direct question that conventional title-only screening could miss: whether the person can use implementation-level prototypes to discover uncertainties and turn the resulting learning into reusable production practice. The evidence is relevant even without shared industry or AI vocabulary, though conventional portfolio review might also capture it.

**Strongest Falsification Argument:** The account reports improved questions rather than a clear user or business outcome, leaves individual ownership and technical depth unclear, and concerns agency workflow rather than AI-assisted expert operations. Collision-01 may have treated activity at the design-development boundary as proof of effective end-to-end product change.

**Verdict:** SURVIVES

**Why:** The employer explicitly seeks production prototyping and continued component-system development, and P012 provides a specific, same-medium mechanism for changing design-development work rather than an analogy based on generic iteration. Attribution and outcome strength remain investigation targets, but they do not erase the demonstrated participation in repeated implementation-level process change.

## Cross-Relationship Findings

### 1. What abstraction pattern caused the most false-positive pressure?

The dominant pattern was converting a shared tension into an asserted meta-capability: cautious change under uncertainty, repeatability without erasing variation, or transparency with trust. Once phrased abstractly, very different evidence could appear relevant while omitting the intervention ability needed between the past outcome and Patch's state change. “Observed a tension and made a context-appropriate adjustment” was especially prone to collapsing into generic adaptability.

### 2. Did Collision-01 confuse any shared semantic tension with transferable capability?

Yes. P003 confused bounded intervention in physical conservation with the ability to sequence reversible software productization. P008 confused a team response to stated menu preferences with the ability to determine boundaries between platform standards and expert discretion. P006 partially made the same error by moving from an organizational retrospective system to encoding expert practice in product software. P010 retained a concrete mechanism but overstated the Patch-side evidence that the corresponding governance problem exists.

### 3. Which relationships, if any, contain information that conventional recruiting representations would likely discard?

P004 contains a conditional, claim-level accountability practice—linking outward quantitative statements to rerunnable support while recognizing sensitivity limits—that role titles or broad skill labels would likely flatten. P010 contains a specific interaction between policy visibility, control bypass, and operator burden that a museum-sector label could hide, although the relationship remains weak. P006's multi-perspective evidence-to-action loop may also be lost in a generic process or program label, but its product transfer and individual ownership remain weak. P012's mechanism is meaningful, but code-based prototyping, front-end style guides, and design-development work are more likely to survive conventional portfolio or experience representation.

### 4. What evidence was missing most often when trying to distinguish analogy from transfer?

The most frequent absence was person-level evidence of exercising the intermediate decision ability required at Patch: not merely participating in a team change, but personally framing the relevant uncertainty, choosing among interventions, and observing a consequence. Also repeatedly missing were a comparable second context showing the capability survives a domain change, and employer-side evidence of the concrete current workflow or failure mode that the proposed intervention would change.
