# Layer 3 — Evidence Reconstruction

This reconstruction is bounded by the sources in Layer 1 and the employer expression in Layer 2. Labels describe epistemic status, not hiring fields.

## Directly Supported

- Patch publicly describes itself as an AI-native services company serving enterprise environmental-resource procurement across multiple markets. [S3]
- Patch publicly describes an operating model that combines embedded human expertise, proprietary or centralized market data, and software across strategy, sourcing, diligence, purchase, and ongoing management. [S2, S3, S4]
- The 2025 platform announcement describes fragmented and non-standardized data, trust-sensitive diligence, resource-intensive workflows, time pressure around inventory, and error-prone portfolio administration as conditions its platform addresses. [S4]
- The hiring page says present delivery includes dashboards, decks, and analyst workflows, while future surfaces may include conversational interfaces, agents, or formats not yet defined. [S1]
- The hiring page identifies a dedicated product-design vacancy intended to work directly with engineering, climate strategists, brand design, leadership, and customers. [S1]
- The work spans both internal and customer-facing surfaces. The page explicitly calls for observing manual strategist work and considering which parts should become platform capabilities. [S1]
- The employer expects the person to frame problems, design, prototype or ship production code, lead initiatives through delivery, expose AI contributions in a trustworthy way, and continue developing the design language and component system. [S1]
- The stated near-term outcome is meaningful improvement to existing workflows and relationship-building with strategists and customers during the first 90 days. [S1]
- The posting explicitly states San Francisco, on-site, full-time work and a $170K–$240K base range with equity. [S1]

## Inferred

- **Inference:** Patch appears to be moving from a set of human-led and partly manual delivery practices toward a more unified product experience in which recurring expert work becomes software-supported or software-mediated. This follows from S1's contrast between present workflows and future interfaces, reinforced by S2–S4's combined service model.
- **Inference:** A central design tension is likely to be increasing repeatability and throughput without hiding judgment, provenance, uncertainty, or risk in a trust-sensitive domain. S1 explicitly asks for visible and trustworthy AI contributions; S3–S4 emphasize rigor, trust, fragmented data, and human expertise.
- **Inference:** The dedicated nature of the role suggests product design is becoming a more concentrated responsibility or organizational priority, but the evidence does not establish whether this is the first designer the company has ever employed. [S1]
- **Inference:** Rapid feedback between builders and climate strategists is intended to turn live service experience into product changes on a short cycle. The exact cadence and decision authority remain unstated. [S1, S2]
- **Inference:** The organization wants a person who can reduce handoff distance between design and implementation. This is supported by the end-to-end ownership and production-prototyping language, but the current handoff process is not described. [S1]

## Unknown

- Why the vacancy exists now: new headcount, replacement, reorganization, workload increase, or another cause.
- The current design-team composition, reporting line, decision rights, and whether “dedicated product designer” means the only product designer.
- Current customer count or revenue attributable to the workflows discussed in the posting.
- Which specific workflow or product surface has the highest priority after the first 90 days.
- The actual proportion of strategist work that is manual, automated, experimental, or already productionized.
- Which AI capabilities are live, their reliability, their model or data architecture, and how trust is measured.
- How customer research access, consent, confidentiality, and environmental-market data rights are handled.
- Concrete quality, adoption, productivity, revenue, climate-impact, or safety metrics for the role.
- Whether dashboards, decks, conversational interfaces, and agents will coexist or whether any are intended to replace others.
- The vacancy's publication date and closing date; the source was publicly indexed near retrieval, but the posting itself did not expose those dates in the accessible representation.
- Whether the 2025 platform framing still describes every aspect of internal operations in September 2026.

## Reconstruction limitations

- The reconstruction relies entirely on Patch's public self-description; there is no independent observation of internal work.
- Marketing and hiring pages may state aspirations alongside current reality. Where the sources blur those, this file avoids upgrading aspiration into fact.
- The older platform announcement is treated as background continuity, not as proof of the immediate trigger for the vacancy.
- The evidence supports an organizational and product situation, but not causal claims about headcount, performance, urgency, customer demand, or internal deficiencies.
- No person-side evidence was consulted, so the reconstruction makes no claim about availability, fit, scarcity, or matchability.
