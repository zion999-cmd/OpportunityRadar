# Semantic Native Real-Evidence Pilot 01

## Acquisition protocol

This directory contains a demand-independent acquisition sample of public professional material. Selection was based on whether a public source documented a concrete situation, responsibility or action, decision, and/or outcome. Selection did not use resumes, skill tags, job taxonomies, candidate profiles, employer situations, target requirements, recruiting results, relationship outputs, or matching prompts.

Sources were limited to material accessible without authentication and carrying clear public provenance. No private contact information or unrelated personal information was collected. Evidence notes are short factual paraphrases; source text was not copied except where a minimal excerpt would be necessary. No excerpts were necessary in this pilot.

Diversity was pursued by varying the kind of real-world event described, not by imposing occupational quotas. No domain contributes more than two admitted samples, and no more than two admitted samples center on essentially the same kind of event.

Every plausible page deliberately evaluated for inclusion is recorded in `acquisition-candidates.ndjson`, including methodological rejection reasons. The admitted-source ledger is `acquisition-log.ndjson`.
