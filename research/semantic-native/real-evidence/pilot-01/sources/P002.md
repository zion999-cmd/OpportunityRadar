## Raw Professional Evidence

Dawid Ostręga describes coaching the Krakow Kings after merging underperforming teams. A highly directive approach preceded a championship, but the following season ended with only three wins. An anonymous survey from roughly 50 players attributed anger and dependency to his control. He concluded that nominal captains had not been given meaningful responsibility, then shifted decision-making toward captains and peer accountability and changed how he delivered feedback. The page reports that the team later regained the championship. The site's transcript notes are edited from automatic captions rather than presented as a verbatim transcript.
