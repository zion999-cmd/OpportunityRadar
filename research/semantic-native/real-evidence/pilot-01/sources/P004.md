## Raw Professional Evidence

Jeremy Singer-Vine describes work supporting a seven-month investigation based on interviews, documents, and two government datasets. The team published its data, methodology, and analysis code alongside the story. For this project, it connected each quantitative statement to supporting code, placed a readable methodology before code, made the workflow rerunnable, and sought review of assumptions and clarity from colleagues and subject-matter experts. Singer-Vine reports that preparing for public scrutiny encouraged clearer analysis and additional fact-checking. He also identifies sensitive data as a reason not to publish a fully reproducible package, showing that the decision was conditional rather than automatic.
