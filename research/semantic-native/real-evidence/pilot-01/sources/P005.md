## Raw Professional Evidence

Simon Collison recounts an internal redesign undertaken after Erskine Design grew from two people to eight and its existing site no longer represented the firm or supported useful inquiries. The team treated the internal effort as client work, naming project and design leads, tracking it in existing project systems, and maintaining a shared physical work area. They wrote and selected content before iterating through sketches, wireframes, and an early content-system prototype that exposed missing functionality. After launch, Collison reports that the site could accommodate evolving content and that incoming proposals contained richer information and were of higher quality. These outcomes are the author's observations rather than an independently audited evaluation.
