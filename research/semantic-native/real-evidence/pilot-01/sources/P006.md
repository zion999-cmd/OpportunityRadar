## Raw Professional Evidence

Debra Lavell describes an eight-year effort beginning in 2002 to make project retrospectives a repeatable practice at Intel. The initial situation included geographically dispersed teams, little or no travel budget, cultural differences, resistance to change, inconsistent documentation, weak retrieval of past lessons, and no reliable loop from findings to action. The adopted process gathered project data, reconstructed events from multiple perspectives, created and communicated action plans, and stored findings centrally. The paper reports approximately 500 retrospectives across internal teams during the period and includes participant-reported benefits. Its strongest outcome claims are practitioner estimates and interview reports, not controlled measurements.
