## Raw Professional Evidence

Catherine T. Osgood recounts work on Skylab rendezvous and launch planning. After damage to the workshop during launch, the team needed an option that allowed the next vehicle to launch across a longer window rather than depend on first-day rendezvous. Osgood had previously developed and advocated a longer-window plan; the in-flight problem made that contingency newly useful. She describes running repeated mainframe jobs under priority and remaining near the workplace so results and decisions could be turned around quickly. Her recollection is that the plan ultimately may not have been used, but it was available if required. The interview explicitly credits a larger team and preserves Osgood's uncertainty about some details decades later.
