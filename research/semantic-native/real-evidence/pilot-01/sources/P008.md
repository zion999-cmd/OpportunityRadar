## Raw Professional Evidence

Charles T. Bourland recounts a design change in the Skylab food system. The initial plan gave every crew member the same menu, but repeated crew tastings showed that individuals did not want identical selections. The team changed the plan so each person could choose a menu. Bourland also describes collaboration with the Natick Laboratories, including canned bread production and review of new food products. The transcript establishes the feedback-driven change but does not provide a quantitative comparison of consumption or mission outcomes before and after it.
