## Raw Professional Evidence

Benjamin Weber describes a four-person team producing a game demo in 480 person-hours. Anticipating excessive scope, the team moved nonessential work to a wish list, completed essential tasks early, and used remaining time for polish. The team nevertheless faced an unstable in-house engine, late engine changes that prevented packaging the planned installer, incomplete preproduction, and occasional overtime caused by accepting assets after a lock. Repeated playtests exposed layout and communication problems and settled an internal disagreement when players avoided a proposed risk-reward feature; the design was changed in response. The project shipped with setup instructions instead of the intended installer.
