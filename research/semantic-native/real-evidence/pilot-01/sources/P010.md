## Raw Professional Evidence

Jessica Keyes describes designing a visitor-response station for a Baltimore Museum of Art exhibition expected to provoke strong reactions. She and a colleague chose an inclusive prompt and a Rolodex display intended to avoid ranking responses, then created a two-question moderation rule. After three weeks they made that rule visible to improve transparency. Staff reviewed 739 submissions; 600 were displayed, and only two were rejected as hateful or profane. Visitors could bypass moderation by inserting cards directly, forcing staff to inspect the full display twice weekly. Keyes reports that the results supported confronting difficult topics more directly while also identifying changes needed for later response stations. Counts and interpretations are the author's informal review.
