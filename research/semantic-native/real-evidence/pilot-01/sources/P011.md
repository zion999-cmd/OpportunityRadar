## Raw Professional Evidence

Johanna Chun and Diana Ostberg describe applying a lightweight retrospective to a recurring weekly staff meeting at Saam Architecture. The meeting had become habitual even as organizational needs changed. Their team examined how its structure and content supported or hindered shared purpose, tested targeted changes, and reshaped the meeting to better support engagement and learning. The public conference description characterizes the work as an evolving case study and does not supply quantitative before-and-after measures, so the outcome is limited to the speakers' reported operational change and observations.
