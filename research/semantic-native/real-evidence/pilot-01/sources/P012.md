## Raw Professional Evidence

Mark Llobrera describes a digital agency using a museum website redesign to test several new ways of connecting design and development. Rather than declare a complete new process in advance, the team introduced modular content and layout, front-end style guides, and code-based wireframes across three projects, recording how each change affected time, collaborators, and clients. Rough prototypes on a later literary-magazine project helped the developer and designer ask better questions, and lessons from the experiments were carried into another colleague's project. The agency subsequently made sharing lessons from completed projects part of a monthly all-staff meeting. The account emphasizes that methods were still being refined.
